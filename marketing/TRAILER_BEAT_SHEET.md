# LAST CALL — Trailer Beat Sheets
*Teaser (0:60) + Theatrical Trailer (2:20). Every line below exists in the
screenplay — the campaign never writes jokes the movie doesn't cash.*

Campaign rule: the trailers are cut in the DOCUSERIES voice. The marketing
pretends to be *Rust Belt Outlaws* the same way the film does — the audience
gets the joke of the movie before they've bought a ticket, and the format is
infinitely clippable for social.

---

## TEASER — "THE PERFECT CRIME. IN REVERSE." (0:60)

**0:00–0:12** — Black. Ominous synth. Drone over dead main street (SC 1.1).
NARRATOR: "Maumee Flats, Ohio... the epicenter of a crime wave that authorities
are still struggling to explain."

**0:12–0:18** — HARD CUT: Sheriff Paczki talking head. "Struggling to explain?
No. It's Dale and Ricky. It's been Dale and Ricky since 1994."

**0:18–0:26** — Prestige heist grammar: gliding steadicam, split diopter on the
padlock, tense strings — two men in the storage unit... **placing a duffel bag
gently INSIDE.** Ricky in the ski mask. DALE: "Why are you wearing the mask NOW?
We're giving it BACK."

**0:26–0:34** — QUICK HITS: the urn drop in slow motion / ash cloud / RICKY
(tiny, muffled): "...Ope." / smash to black.

**0:34–0:46** — LARRY (warm as syrup), thermos unscrewing mixed like a gunshot:
"I'm not mad, fellas." DALE (under breath): "Oh no." LARRY: "I'm disappointed."

**0:46–0:54** — TITLE STING over synth: **LAST CALL.** Then Mrs. Halversen's
talking head, sweet as pie: "D-minus students. Both of them."

**0:54–0:60** — Button: Mr. Business, locked-off frame, staring. Beat. The whole
bar (O.S.), murmured: "Mr. Business." CARD: *The perfect crime. In reverse.*

*Social cutdowns from this teaser alone: the un-robbery (0:15), "I'm not mad"
(0:12), Mr. Business (0:06 loop). Three platforms fed by one edit.*

## THEATRICAL TRAILER — "GIVE IT BACK. GET IT BACK. GIVE IT BACK AGAIN." (2:20)

**ACT 1 OF TRAILER (0:00–0:45) — the world and the mistake**
- Doc open compressed: drone + NARRATOR ("...a crime wave...") + Paczki
  ("Nobody wants to do the paperwork.")
- Walmart lot: the wave. RICKY: "A wave says 'nothing criminal is occurring.'"
- The unit: cash reveal. RICKY: "How much is that?" DALE (flat): "More than
  forty dollars."
- The urn engraving. DALE (very quiet): "We robbed Polite Larry."
- Paczki: "Twenty-two years, I have never heard that man raise his voice."

**ACT 2 OF TRAILER (0:45–1:30) — the deal and the spiral**
- Flapjack booth. LARRY: "Try the boysenberry. People sleep on the boysenberry."
  → "...and you two are going to drive it there... no stops, no detours..."
- Montage on Kevin's diss track (the actual track — the movie's music IS the
  trailer music): truck rolling / casino sign in Ricky's sunglasses / "DOM-I-NIC!"
  chant / Dale at the birthday party handing Emma the wrapped catalytic
  converter. EMMA: "It's HEAVY!"
- The turn: empty parking space. RICKY (small): "The Jessups." DALE: "The
  Jessup twins have Larry Gunderson's truck." RICKY: "When you say the whole
  sentence like that it sounds bad."
- CRYSTAL (to both of them, deadpan): "Now go get the pancake man's truck
  before he politely ends you."

**ACT 3 OF TRAILER (1:30–2:05) — the guys get good (sell the convergence)**
- Music shift: synth parody drops away, real score enters — the trailer itself
  converges, like the film.
- Sober Dale casing the compound (machine-online monologue, clipped): "There
  are two of them and three of us..." RICKY (horrified whisper): "He's like this
  NOW? What have I been drinking WITH all these years?"
- Heist flashes: Say Anything laptop over Ricky's head / SHORT DARRYL: "IT
  DOESN'T EVEN SCAN! THE METER'S ALL WRONG!" / Tall Darryl stepping out hands up:
  "You look different, man." DALE: "I feel different."
- One breath of sincerity, dawn on the bridge (P2 wide, 2 seconds — sell that
  the movie has a heart without spending it): DALE: "And Emma?"
- The ATV sinking into the pond. SHORT DARRYL (to the sky): "WE DID THE FORKLIFT
  THING! THAT WAS US!"

**BUTTON RUN (2:05–2:20)**
- TITLE: **LAST CALL**
- Larry, pleasant as ever: "Fellas — get the paperwork." RICKY (hand on heart):
  "Larry, we accept your syrup."
- FINAL BUTTON: Paczki talking head. "Never charged. Yeah." (beat; glances off
  camera) "...They do a two-for-one on Thursdays."

**WHAT THE TRAILER NEVER SHOWS (protected reveals):** Duke-in-the-urn, the Tommy
backstory, the flask/ball-pit hold, the goose lady's value, the sobriety chip,
and the entire ending. The trailer sells the premise and the voice; the movie
keeps its heart and its third-act turns. Audiences reward comedies whose best
90 seconds AREN'T all in the trailer — that's how you get an A– CinemaScore and
a sub-45% second-weekend drop.

   disappointed."*
