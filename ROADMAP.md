## REQUIRES THE PHYSICAL WORLD (the producer's lane, roughly in order)

1. **Chain of title:** register the screenplay (WGA + copyright) — costs ~$45
2. **Package:** attach producer → director → one bankable cast anchor
   (the DECISIONS_LOCKED first-offer board is the target list)
3. **Finance:** $4.5M plan per BUDGET_SCHEDULE (equity + Ohio 30% incentive +
   presales); or the $3M descope ladder
4. **Pre-production:** 8 weeks — casting, locations (NW Ohio scout list is in
   VISUAL_BIBLE §7), crew, the raccoon
5. **Shoot:** 25 days per the block schedule
6. **Post:** ~26 weeks per the post calendar
7. **Festival → sale → release:** per RELEASE_STRATEGY (SXSW target,
   Toledo-before-Manhattan platform plan)

**The realistic near-term win:** items 3–5 of "buildable here" make this repo
a submission-grade package — the thing that gets producers, agents, and
festival labs (Sundance Labs, Austin Film Festival screenplay competition) to
say yes. That's how indie films actually start.
