## STANDING DIRECTIVES (from the producer, binding on all future work)

1. **Full authority delegated:** the director chooses among options; every
   choice optimizes for critical acclaim × box-office performance ("a
   proficient creative director who wants to sell this movie with high ratings
   at the box office").
2. **Marketing is concurrent, not subsequent:** while creating the movie,
   always think in trailers, marketing strategies, and short-form clips.
   Implemented as: `[CLIP]` tags at shot-list level, the Social B-Body capture
   system, the protected-scene register, and the innovation ledger.
3. **No shortcuts that cut performance:** social/marketing capture never costs
   a take, a setup, or a minute of actor performance; if marketing and the
   film conflict, the film wins (the tone IS the product).
4. **Compounding quality:** every deliverable must improve on the last —
   maintained via the Innovation Ledger (CLIP_MAP §4) and this log.
