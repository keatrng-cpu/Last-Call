## CONFLICTS ADJUDICATED (director's rulings)

1. **The flask pour-out:** heart said cliché, comedy said necessary. RULING:
   pour it — the deadpan inoculates the cliché ("It was well whiskey, Ricky.
   It knew what it was."), it completes the foley arc (drink → almost →
   surrender → chip), and it rhymes with Ricky's wordless baggie flick: both
   vices into the same river, four minutes apart.
2. **Dottie's rent gag:** heart offered it as optional. RULING: cut — the
   finale oner already absorbs five new beats; the count payoff is the one
   that matters.
3. **Q3 timeline:** comedy's line said "fourteen months"; script says eleven.
   Corrected to "month nine" on application.
