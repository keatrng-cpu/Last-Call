*Master Director Connector v1.0. Completes SHOT_LIST_ACT_ONE.md. Same grammar
rules (Language A/B, deadpan push, chaos cuts, Larry vise).*

**NEW STANDING SYSTEM — `[CLIP]` tags:** any setup tagged `[CLIP]` is a
designated short-form marketing asset. For those setups only, a second body
captures a simultaneous 9:16 vertical frame (see marketing/CLIP_MAP.md for the
rig and the release calendar). Clips are planned at the shot level so the
campaign never cannibalizes a protected reveal and never costs a performance
take — social capture rolls WITH the take, never instead of it.

## SC 17 — INT. SIR CHEESE-A-LOT - AFTERNOON (intercut with 16)

- **17.1** Entrance wide, 28mm: Dale with gift bag, a desaturated man in a
  primary-colored riot. Animatronic mouse mid-frame, mid-song, terrible.
- **17.2** THE BALL-PIT HOLD: 50mm, sticks, Dale beside the ball pit — flask
  out, cap off (foley forward: the squeak), Emma through the glass, cap back
  on. LONGEST HOLD IN THE FILM. No score. No cut. Protected: never in any
  campaign material — this is the film's soul and the trailer never touches it.
- **17.3** Becca beat: handheld singles, close, guarded — her smelling-him
  reflex, the surprise. Intimate coverage, no wides — this scene is private.
- **17.4** THE CONVERTER GIFT: two-shot kneel, 35mm; Emma's "It's HEAVY!" gets
  her own single. `[CLIP — with Becca's "What is wrong with you?" /
  "Everything. Working on it." as the button]`
- **17.5** Becca's snort-laugh: HER single, 50mm — the casting-priority beat.
  One involuntary crack of light. Protect the take with air around it.
- **17.6** THE EXIT: widest frame of Act Two, 21mm — one man walking out
  through a sea of birthday joy, mouse singing him out. Comedy framing,
  tragedy content. Score: the mouse's song only.
- **INTERCUT PLAN (16/17):** cut on motion-matches — Emma bounces/dice bounce;
  candles/chips; Dale kneels/Ricky bows to the table. The edit is the essay.
