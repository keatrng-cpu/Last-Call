# LAST CALL — Budget & Schedule Rough
*Indie-comedy scale. Numbers are development-stage top-sheet estimates for
conversation, not a locked budget. Assumes Ohio shoot with state incentive.*

---

## THE HEADLINE

- **Target budget:** $4.5M all-in ($3.2M direct + contingency/finance costs),
  before incentive recoupment.
- **Shoot:** 25 days, single unit + 2 drone days + 1 splinter day.
- **Where the money shows on screen:** cast, the finale one-take, the dawn bridge
  hour, and one needle-drop. Everywhere else the film is *supposed* to look cheap
  — the aesthetic subsidizes the budget (Language B's whole grammar is
  affordable on purpose; the doc's "prestige" is a slider, a drone, and a synth
  patch).

**Descope ladder if financing lands at $3M:** (1) leads take backend-weighted
deals; (2) crane days → slider; (3) needle-drop → score-only dawn drive (last
resort — this is the film's one music promise); (4) casino becomes 1 day in a
smaller card room. The raccoon is not on the descope ladder. Mr. Business is
load-bearing.

## SCHEDULE ROUGH — 25 DAYS (5-day weeks, NW Ohio, shoot fall for gray skies)

**Week 1 — The Squeaky Wheel block (Days 1–5)**
All bar interiors: Act 1 scenes, back-booth planning, back-room count + Moral
Choice #1 (bulb swing rig pre-built), Dottie/Becca beat. Day 5: rehearse finale
one-take floorplan after wrap.

**Week 2 — Squeaky Wheel finale + town (Days 6–10)**
Day 6: FINALE ONE-TAKE (full day — a stunt, budgeted like one) + flask/chip
coverage. Day 7: bar exteriors, truck send-off, Mr. Business plates (raccoon day
1 of 2). Days 8–9: Walmart parking lot night, town exteriors, Kevin scenes.
Day 10: docuseries talking heads batch 1 (Paczki, Halversen, Gary — one location
redressed 3×, cheapest day of the shoot).

**Week 3 — Flapjack + storage block (Days 11–15)**
Days 11–12: Flapjack Cabin nights after close — both booth scenes (slider vise
rigged once, used twice), Brenda beat. Days 13–14: Stor-Mor weekend — break-in,
un-robbery, urn drop (ash = multiple wardrobe doubles ready), Larry's cocoa
arrival. Day 15: truck process/hood-mount work — bickering montage, highway fork.

**Week 4 — Toledo split block (Days 16–20)**
Days 16–17: casino (real card room or dressed Elks lodge; "DOM-I-NIC" crowd =
50 extras one evening), lounge scene with Crystal. Day 18: Sir Cheese-A-Lot
(rent a family fun center Monday-dark; animatronic mouse is a costume + puppet
head). Day 19: parking garage — Crystal's protected scene, scheduled with air
around it; this is a performance day, not a page-count day. Day 20: talking
heads batch 2 (Brenda, Darryl in camo curtains) + inserts.

**Week 5 — The convergence (Days 21–25)**
Days 21–23: Jessup compound heist (pre-dawn calls; crane days 22–23; ATV sink
morning of 23 with recovery rigging pre-set). Day 24: THE BRIDGE — dawn window
(7 setups in ~75 min, boards P2/P8/P11 as one setup), afternoon: truck cargo-box
interior (goose lady discovery) + dawn-drive plates. Day 25: drone unit final
day, pickups, weather-cover queue.

**Post:** 14-week editor's cut → locked at week 22 → 4 weeks sound (the mix IS
several of the jokes: ambience dropouts, thermos-as-gunshot, mono Kevin) →
2-week DI with the two-LUT grade → festival print.

**Schedule risks flagged early:** dawn bridge is single-window (Day 24 has Day 25
as weather cover); raccoon days are immovable once booked; casino extras night
needs local radio push two weeks out; child actor (Emma) hours cap Day 18 —
shoot her out first.
