## Directing Principles (non-negotiable)

- **Tone:** 70% sharp comedy / 30% real grief. Every joke about the drinking and the
  coke is a floorboard over something true. Act 3 pulls the boards up.
- **The gap is the joke:** prestige true-crime filmmaking laid over flat Rust Belt
  reality. In Act 3 the two styles converge — reality finally earns the cool shot.
- **Moral choice beats** are shot wide, held long, and scored with silence:
  the back room bulb-swing, the highway fork, the bridge.
- **Deadpan gets the slow push-in. Chaos gets the quick cut.** Larry gets one
  continuous, imperceptible tightening frame per scene.
- **Color:** desaturated gray/brown town; The Squeaky Wheel is the only amber
  until the earned golden-hour dawn drive.
